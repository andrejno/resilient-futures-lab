Responsible AI use / Worked example

Check the claim.
Keep the useful part.

The example below was invented for learning. It is not an output attributed to a particular AI service.

“At 5% annual inflation, a basket costing €80 today will cost €84 in two years. So you will need exactly €4 more.”

1 / Split it up

There is a stated starting price (€80), a rate (5% a year), a period (two years) and a conclusion (€84). The answer assumes the same basket and constant inflation, but uses only one year of price growth.

2 / Recalculate

After year 1: €80 × 1.05 = €84.00
After year 2: €84 × 1.05 = €88.20

The modelled two-year increase is €8.20, not €4. Saying “exactly” is also too strong for a forecast of real prices: the constant 5% rate is an assumption.

3 / Check the concept

The ECB’s inflation explainer describes broad price growth and its effect on purchasing power. It does not predict that this particular basket will rise by 5% every year. General inflation and your own spending pattern can differ.

4 / Write a better answer

“If this basket rises by 5% each year for two years, its modelled cost is €80 × 1.05² = €88.20. That is €8.20 more than today. Actual prices can change differently; this is a fixed-rate example.”

Practice: choose what to share

Possible inputSafer choice for this exercise

A public article you want summarisedProvide a link or an appropriate excerpt, state the task, and check the summary against the original.
